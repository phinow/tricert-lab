## PMP (PMI). Source: PMI PMP Examination Content Outline, July 2026 (pmi.org PDF) and pmi.org PMP page
- Re-checked 10 November 2026 (search results of pmi.org only; the pages were not opened). The domain weights (and the old 42/50/8) and the People and Business Environment task counts were seen again. The Process task count and the 9 July date were not seen again; they stand from the 18 October check. Pull-down list items and paper delivery were not seen again: do not build lessons or questions on them.
- New exam in effect since July 9, 2026.
- A copy of the outline on pmi.org is labelled revised 11 August 2026. What changed from the July copy was not found. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- 180 questions: 170 scored, 10 unscored pretest. 240 minutes. Two 10-minute breaks.
- Breaks: the first comes after the case-study section; the second about midway through the independent questions. The number of case studies is not published. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Item types: case or scenario, graphic-based, multiple-choice single response, multiple response; computer-based only: enhanced matching, point and click, matching, pull-down list.
- Case or scenario and graphic-based are new. Case or scenario: a detailed scenario, possibly with visuals, followed by a series of questions that use all of it. Graphic-based: interpret a chart, graph, diagram or image. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Delivery: test centre (computer or paper) or online proctored.
- Approaches: about 40% of items represent predictive approaches; the other 60% is divided between adaptive (agile) and hybrid. Approaches run through all three domains and are not tied to a task. The exact number per item type and approach may vary by form. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- AI and sustainability appear as scenario context, not as separate topics: questions may include AI tools such as dashboards and other project artifacts; sustainability sits inside planning, quality, compliance and risk decisions. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Domains: People 33% (8 tasks), Process 41% (10 tasks), Business Environment 26% (8 tasks).
- People tasks: 1 Develop a common vision; 2 Manage conflicts; 3 Lead the project team; 4 Engage stakeholders; 5 Align stakeholder expectations; 6 Manage stakeholder expectations; 7 Help ensure knowledge transfer; 8 Plan and manage communication.
- Process tasks: 1 Develop an integrated project management plan and plan delivery; 2 Develop and manage project scope; 3 Help ensure value-based delivery; 4 Plan and manage resources; 5 Plan and manage procurement; 6 Plan and manage finance; 7 Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables; 8 Plan and manage schedule; 9 Evaluate project status; 10 Manage project closure.
- Business Environment tasks: 1 Define and establish project governance; 2 Plan and manage project compliance; 3 Manage and control changes; 4 Remove impediments and manage issues; 5 Plan and manage risk; 6 Continuous improvement; 7 Support organizational change; 8 Evaluate external business environment changes.
- Eligibility (experience within the last 10 years before the application is submitted, non-overlapping): secondary school 60 months; associate's or post-secondary vocational 48 months; bachelor's or equivalent 36 months; bachelor's or postgraduate from a PMI GAC-accredited program 24 months. Plus 35 contact hours of project management training (waived for active CAPM holders).
- Education tiers as worded in the 2026 outline: secondary diploma 60 months; associate-level, higher certificate or advanced technical or vocational program (EQF level 5 / ISCED 5) 48 months; bachelor's or higher 36 months; a degree from a PMI GAC-accredited program 24 months. Older PMI pages still say 8 years; the 2026 outline says 10. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Months count once: two projects in the same months are not counted twice. Tenure is tracked in months, and each project is recorded separately. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- The experience must be leading and directing projects. The title does not decide it: a coordinator, analyst or team lead may qualify by showing they led and directed project work, and a project manager title does not make routine work count. Routine, operational or administrative tasks do not count. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Unpaid work can count if it is in a professional setting. School projects, academic research for a degree, planning personal events and home improvement do not count. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Each project description on the application is limited to 550 characters. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- The 35 hours must be aligned with the outline and finished before the application is submitted. Hours from several qualifying courses can be added together. An active CAPM waives the 35 hours only, not the experience months. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Self-paced, on-demand training from any organization counts if it is aligned to the current outline, is formal project management instruction, states its hours, has an end-of-course assessment and gives a completion record. Books, practice exams and other self-directed study do not count, so study in this app gives none of the 35 hours. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- From 1 December 2026, live instructor-led training counts toward the 35 hours only if delivered by a PMI Authorized Training Partner, a China Registered Education Provider, or an eligible accredited higher-education program.
- Courses finished before 1 December 2026 stay eligible under the old rule even if the application comes later. Self-paced training is not affected by the change. One PMI blog says "late Q4 2026" instead of the date. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- PMI's own self-paced course, PMI Authorized On-demand PMP Exam Prep (EL034), meets the 35 hours and was listed at US$799 regular price on one listing; member price not seen. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on the pmi.org shop page.)
- Exam fee: US$405 for PMI members, US$655 for non-members. The member price applies only if membership is active before you pay. Regional pricing applies; no Canadian dollar figure was seen. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Not found on 10 November 2026, so do not state them: PMI membership dues, the PMP retake fee (PMI says only "a substantial discount"), a Canadian dollar price, and the PMP pass mark.
- Application and audit, from PMI's certification handbooks (probably true for PMP, not shown PMP-specific): review of an online application takes 5 calendar days; any application can be selected for audit, and you are told by email before payment; in an audit you have 90 days to send documents (copy of diploma or degree, a supervisor or manager signature for each project, training certificates), processed in about 5 to 7 business days. Do not state how long an unsubmitted application stays open. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- After approval there is a 1-year eligibility period with up to 3 attempts. After an audit, the year starts when the audit is passed. Rescheduling or cancelling within 30 days of the appointment costs US$70 plus tax (generic certification handbook, revised June 2026). (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)
- Renewal: 60 PDUs every 3 years.
- Of the 60 PDUs, at least 35 are Education and at most 25 Giving Back. Renewal fee US$60 for members and US$150 for non-members, subject to regional pricing; these amounts may be dated. (Seen in search results of pmi.org on 10 November 2026; confirm on pmi.org.)

## PL-300 Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst. Source: Microsoft Learn study guide (skills current as of April 20, 2026), certification page, exam duration page, FAQ, retake policy and renewal pages
- Re-checked 10 November 2026; wording confirmed on the Microsoft Learn pages on 11 November 2026. The skills list is still "as of April 20, 2026" and no later date is announced. The April 20 change log marks a "Minor" change in three sub-areas only (Get or connect to data; Enhance reports for usability and storytelling; Create and manage workspaces and assets).
- Certification: Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate.
- 100 minutes. Passing score 700 (scaled). Proctored via Pearson VUE; may include interactive components. Microsoft does not publish a fixed question count for PL-300; do not state one. Its general statement is that most Microsoft exams have 40 to 60 questions and the number varies by exam.
- Seat time: allow 120 minutes. Microsoft lists 100 minutes of exam time and 120 minutes of seat time for associate exams without labs (instructions, candidate agreement and comments). It does not list which exams have labs.
- Case studies are not timed separately. Once you leave a case study you cannot go back to its questions.
- Microsoft Learn can be opened during associate and expert exams (not Fundamentals). Microsoft says it is for questions where you may need to look something up, not for every question.
- Breaks: 5 minutes are built into the exam time; the clock keeps running during a break, and you cannot return to questions you saw before the break.
- Exam sandbox: a free demo of the exam interface. Microsoft does not name the question types of an exam in advance.
- Practice Assessment: free. The PL-300 certification page points to a new Practice Assessment on AI Skills Navigator (sign-in needed) that "more closely aligns with the exam experience"; the legacy Practice Assessments on Learn are no longer updated. Microsoft says practice questions are not the same as the exam and do not show its length or complexity, so a practice score is not a pass predictor.
- Fee: Associate exams "typically cost US$165", priced by the country or region where the exam is proctored; taxes may apply. No Canadian dollar price was found.
- Renew every 12 months with a free online assessment on Microsoft Learn.
- Renewal details: the assessment is free, unproctored and open book; take it any time in the six-month eligibility window before expiry; passing extends the certification one year from the expiry date; no limit on tries (after the second try, wait 24 hours between attempts). An expired certification cannot be renewed: you must pass the exam again.
- Retake after 24 hours following a first failed attempt.
- Retake policy: 24 hours after the first fail; a 14-day wait between all later attempts; at most 5 attempts in 12 months from the first attempt; you pay for each retake; a passed exam cannot be retaken unless the certification has expired.
- Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment, or the exam fee is lost. (Microsoft Q&A answer on learn.microsoft.com; Pearson VUE's own page could not be opened.)
- No prerequisite is listed on the certification page. PL-300 is not on Microsoft's retirement list (the only exam listed there to retire is MS-102, on 30 November 2026).
- Microsoft's instructor-led course PL-300T00 is 3 days. Microsoft publishes no recommended self-study hours.
- Skill areas: Prepare the data 25–30%; Model the data 25–30%; Visualize and analyze the data 25–30%; Manage and secure Power BI 15–20%.
- Prepare the data: get or connect to data (identify and connect to data sources or a shared semantic model; change data source settings incl. credentials and privacy levels; choose between DirectLake, DirectQuery and Import; create and modify parameters); profile and clean (evaluate data incl. statistics and column properties; resolve inconsistencies, unexpected or null values, data quality issues; resolve import errors); transform and load (data types; create and transform columns; group and aggregate rows; pivot, unpivot, transpose; convert semi-structured data to a table; create fact and dimension tables; reference vs duplicate queries; merge and append; keys for relationships; configure data loading).
- Model the data: design (table and column properties; role-playing dimensions; cardinality and cross-filter direction; common date table; use cases for calculated columns and calculated tables); DAX (single aggregation measures; CALCULATE; time intelligence; basic statistical functions; semi-additive measures; quick measures; calculated tables or columns; calculation groups); optimize (remove unnecessary rows and columns; identify poor measures, relationships and visuals with Performance Analyzer and DAX query view; reduce granularity).
- Visualize and analyze: create reports (select visual; format; narrative visual with Copilot; themes; conditional formatting; slicing and filtering; Copilot new report page and suggestions; configure page; when to use a paginated report; visual calculations with DAX); usability and storytelling (bookmarks; custom tooltips; interactions; navigation; sorting; sync slicers; Selection pane grouping and layering; drillthrough incl. pages, filters, buttons; export settings; mobile design; personalization; accessibility; automatic page refresh); patterns and trends (Analyze feature; grouping, binning, clustering; AI visuals; reference lines, error bars, forecasting; outliers and anomalies; Copilot to summarize the semantic model).
- Manage and secure: workspaces and assets (create and configure workspace; configure and update an app; publish, import, update items; dashboards; choose distribution method; subscriptions and data alerts; promote or certify; when a gateway is required; scheduled refresh); secure and govern (workspace roles; item-level access; access to semantic models; RLS roles; RLS group membership; sensitivity labels).
- Live wording of some lines (10 November 2026): "Apply sorting to visuals"; "Configure drillthrough navigation, including pages, filters, and buttons"; "Enable personalization in a report, including personalized visuals"; "Configure and update an app".

### Practice set-up for PL-300 (Microsoft Learn product pages, confirmed 11 November 2026)
- Power BI Desktop: Windows 10 or Windows Server 2016 or later, .NET 4.7.2 or later, at least 2 GB RAM (4 GB or more recommended), display at least 1440x900 or 1600x900.
- The Power BI service needs a work or school account; consumer addresses such as Gmail or Hotmail cannot be used directly. Microsoft's route without one is a Microsoft 365 trial, which creates a work account.
- Fabric (Free) licence: content for your own use; no sharing and no publishing to other people's workspaces. Sharing needs Power BI Pro or Premium Per User (PPU).
- Fabric trial: 60 days, configured as an F4 or F64 capacity, up to 1 TB in OneLake. When it ends, workspaces go back to Pro, non-Power BI Fabric items stop being reachable, and content stays stored for 7 days for reactivation on a paid capacity.
- Copilot in Power BI needs a workspace on paid Fabric F2 or higher or Premium P1 or higher. Trial capacities are not supported. The tenant setting is on by default; an extra setting is needed if Azure OpenAI is not available in your region.
- Sensitivity labels: need Pro or PPU and Edit permission; with a Free licence labels can be applied only in My workspace.
- Deployment pipelines need PPU or a capacity; they are not a PL-300 skill line.
- Publish to web: an admin must enable the tenant setting; not available for reports that rely on row-level security; anyone on the internet can view what is published.
- Row-level security restricts only users with the Viewer role; it does not apply to Admin, Member or Contributor. "Test as role" checks the filtering.
- Power BI Report Builder for paginated reports is a separate download.

## CISSP (ISC2). Source: ISC2 CISSP exam outline (effective April 15, 2024), ISC2 Exam Ready booklet, cross-checked
- Re-checked 10 November 2026; wording confirmed on isc2.org on 11 November 2026 (CISSP page, exam outline page, CAT page, experience requirements, Associate page, member policies, exam pricing, after your exam, endorsement, FAQ, Peace of Mind Protection).
- New outline from 15 April 2027: ISC2's CISSP pages carry the notice "Effective April 15, 2027, the CISSP exam will be based on a new exam outline." The 2027 outline is linked from the outline page (English, Chinese, Japanese, German, Spanish) but the file could not be downloaded from here, so its domains and weights are not known. Do not state them.
- CAT is used for all CISSP exams, in all languages: 100–150 items, 3 hours, passing score 700 out of 1000. Each exam contains 25 unscored pretest items, and you cannot tell which they are. Item review is not permitted: you cannot go back to earlier questions.
- If you do not answer at least 75 scored and 25 pretest items within the time, you fail. Results come straight after the exam; a fail report shows weak domains and no numerical score.
- Item format: "multiple choice and advanced item types". ISC2 does not list the advanced types on these pages.
- Languages: Chinese, English, German, Japanese, Spanish. Chinese exams only in set windows.
- Test centre only: Pearson Professional Centers and ISC2-authorized Pearson Select test centers. Not online, not at events or mobile sites.
- Registration: buy the exam in your ISC2 account, then use Schedule to book with Pearson VUE. The name on the form must match your ID exactly or you will not be permitted to sit.
- Domains and weights: 1 Security and Risk Management 16%; 2 Asset Security 10%; 3 Security Architecture and Engineering 13%; 4 Communication and Network Security 13%; 5 Identity and Access Management (IAM) 13%; 6 Security Assessment and Testing 12%; 7 Security Operations 13%; 8 Software Development Security 10%.
- The 2024 outline has 62 numbered objectives (12, 6, 10, 3, 6, 5, 15, 5 by domain). The outline page also has a section "How is AI Security Incorporated into the CISSP Domains?"
- ISC2 lists the CISSP audience as experienced security practitioners, managers and executives (for example CISO, CIO, director of security, IT director or manager, security analyst, manager, auditor, architect or consultant).
- Experience: at least five years cumulative, full-time experience in two or more of the eight domains. One month counts when you worked at least 35 hours a week for four weeks. Part-time work (20 to 34 hours a week) counts: 1,040 hours = 6 months, 2,080 hours = 12 months. Paid or unpaid internships count with documentation on company or organization letterhead (or the registrar's stationery for a school).
- Waiver: up to one year, through either a bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, information technology or a related field, or one credential on ISC2's approved list. The list has 25 credentials, among them CompTIA Security+, CompTIA CySA+, SSCP, CCNA, CISM, CCSP and CGRC. CISA and CRISC are not on the current list.
- Without the experience, passing earns Associate of ISC2; you then have up to six years to gain the required experience.
- Associate of ISC2: annual maintenance fee US$50 and 15 CPE credits a year. Upgrading to certified costs the difference of US$85 once the experience is approved. ISC2 policy: "Associates of ISC2 are NOT certified and may not use any Mark or description other than 'Associate of ISC2'." Breaking this rule may bar the candidate from ever earning any ISC2 certification.
- After passing, submit the certification application (endorsement, or Associate) within nine months of the exam date, or retake the exam. An ISC2-certified professional in good standing must endorse it; if you do not know one, ISC2 can act as endorser. Review typically takes four to six weeks after the endorser finishes. Some applications are audited at random.
- Exam fee: US$749 standard registration (Americas and all other regions not listed). Pricing and taxes depend on the exam location; no Canadian dollar price is shown. Rescheduling US$50; cancelling US$100.
- Retakes: 30 test-free days after the first attempt, 60 after the second, 90 after the third and later; at most 4 attempts in 12 months.
- Peace of Mind Protection: two attempts, both within 180 days of purchase, with a waiting period between them; not refundable. Price not seen.
- Maintenance: 120 CPE credits per 3-year cycle are required (90 Group A, 30 Group A or B). About 40 a year (30 Group A, 10 Group A or B) is suggested, not required. Annual maintenance fee US$135 (one fee covers several ISC2 certifications). A 90-day grace period follows the cycle end.
- SSCP: one year of full-time experience in one or more SSCP domains; an SSCP Associate has two years to earn it.
- Not found on ISC2's pages: a recommended number of study hours. Do not cite one as ISC2's.

## ITIL Foundation (Version 5) (PeopleCert / itil.com)
- Re-checked 10 November 2026; wording confirmed on peoplecert.org on 11 November 2026 (ITIL Foundation (Version 5) page, ITIL FAQ, Take2 page, ITIL 4 Foundation page, Foundation Bridge page). itil.com refused the connection on both days.
- Exam: 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes, closed book, pass mark 65% (26 of 40). Online proctored available. No mandatory training prerequisite: self-study is allowed. Nine languages: English, Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish.
- Purchase options on PeopleCert's page (shown "Incl. VAT", no Canadian dollar price): Exam bundle US$690 (exam voucher, official eBook, Learning Resource Kit); eLearning US$1,029 (adds interactive eLearning and auto-marked sample papers); eLearning+ US$1,338 (adds a free exam re-take and the Official Mock Exam). The official eLearning has 9 hours of interactive content. PeopleCert gives no total study-hour figure.
- Rescheduling: free up to 48 hours before the exam; from 48 hours to 4 hours before, USD 60; after that, USD 120 (plus tax where applicable).
- Take2 re-sit: can be bought only before the exam, up to 15 minutes before the session; valid for 6 months from the first exam date; non-transferable and non-refundable; cannot be bought after a failed exam. Free for PeopleCert Plus members. Price not shown.
- Renewal every 3 years. Three routes: (1) PeopleCert Plus membership and 20 CPD points a year for three years (the product page says 60 CPD points); (2) pass another certification from the same product suite, which for ITIL means any ITIL certification; (3) retake the same certification. Passing any Version 5 certification also renews ITIL 4 certificates.
- ITIL 4 Foundation holders can take the Foundation Bridge (Version 5): 20 questions, 30 minutes, 65%, from US$263. Its planned retirement is 31 December 2027.
- ITIL 4 Foundation is still sold at the same tiers (US$690 / 1,029 / 1,338; 40 questions, 60 minutes, 65%). "The current plan is to sunset all ITIL 4 modules on 31 December 2027." ITIL 4 certificates remain valid prerequisites for Version 5 higher levels.
- Learning objectives: digital product and service management concepts; value co-creation; the four dimensions of product and service management; the ITIL Value System; the ITIL guiding principles; the product and service lifecycle; ITIL's management practices; continual improvement; value stream mapping and management.
- Value co-creation is described as "weighing outcomes, costs, risks, experience and sustainability".
- Four dimensions: Organizations and People; Information and Technology; Partners and Suppliers; Value Streams and Processes.
- ITIL Value System components: guiding principles, governance, value chain activities, management practices, continual improvement. Value chain activities are still a current Version 5 component.
- Seven guiding principles (titles unchanged from ITIL 4): Focus on value; Start where you are; Progress iteratively with feedback; Collaborate and promote visibility; Think and work holistically; Keep it simple and practical; Optimize and automate.
- Product and service lifecycle: PeopleCert calls the items "activities", not stages: "the purpose of each product and service lifecycle management activity; from discovery and design to operation, delivery and support". The eight names Discover, Design, Acquire, Build, Transition, Operate, Deliver, Support come from the 18 October check and itil.com search results; they were not re-opened on 10 November.
- Version 5 is described as "AI-native" and unifies digital product and service management. "Digital experience is a core focus in ITIL (Version 5)." ITIL AI Governance (Version 5) is a separate extension module, not one of the Foundation learning objectives: keep AI at awareness level in Foundation content.
- Management practices: "The 34 ITIL management practices will remain largely the same", now in two groups: 1 Product and Service management practices; 2 General management practices. All 34 practice guides are available only through PeopleCert Plus membership.
- Continual improvement: Version 5 includes a refined continual improvement model. The ITIL 4 model asks seven questions (What is the vision? Where are we now? Where do we want to be? How do we get there? Take action. Did we get there? How do we keep the momentum going?). Present these as the ITIL 4 model that Version 5 refines.
- Do NOT use the ITIL 4 value chain activity names (plan, improve, engage, design and transition, obtain/build, deliver and support) as if they were Version 5 terms. Version 5's own names for its value chain activities were not seen. Do not say the lifecycle replaced the value chain.
- The official syllabus (learning outcomes, weights, questions per area) was not found on peoplecert.org. Do not state area weights.
- PeopleCert's career list for this certificate includes IT Project Manager, Service Owner, IT Service Manager and Service Improvement Manager.
- Jira Service Management has a free plan: "Free forever for 3 agents" (atlassian.com, 10 November 2026).
