## PMP (PMI). Source: PMI PMP Examination Content Outline, July 2026 (pmi.org PDF) and pmi.org PMP page
- New exam in effect since July 9, 2026.
- 180 questions: 170 scored, 10 unscored pretest. 240 minutes. Two 10-minute breaks.
- Item types: case or scenario, graphic-based, multiple-choice single response, multiple response; computer-based only: enhanced matching, point and click, matching, pull-down list.
- Delivery: test centre (computer or paper) or online proctored.
- Domains: People 33% (8 tasks), Process 41% (10 tasks), Business Environment 26% (8 tasks).
- People tasks: 1 Develop a common vision; 2 Manage conflicts; 3 Lead the project team; 4 Engage stakeholders; 5 Align stakeholder expectations; 6 Manage stakeholder expectations; 7 Help ensure knowledge transfer; 8 Plan and manage communication.
- Process tasks: 1 Develop an integrated project management plan and plan delivery; 2 Develop and manage project scope; 3 Help ensure value-based delivery; 4 Plan and manage resources; 5 Plan and manage procurement; 6 Plan and manage finance; 7 Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables; 8 Plan and manage schedule; 9 Evaluate project status; 10 Manage project closure.
- Business Environment tasks: 1 Define and establish project governance; 2 Plan and manage project compliance; 3 Manage and control changes; 4 Remove impediments and manage issues; 5 Plan and manage risk; 6 Continuous improvement; 7 Support organizational change; 8 Evaluate external business environment changes.
- Eligibility (experience within the last 10 years, non-overlapping): secondary school 60 months; associate's or post-secondary vocational 48 months; bachelor's or equivalent 36 months; bachelor's or postgraduate from a PMI GAC-accredited program 24 months. Plus 35 contact hours of project management training (waived for active CAPM holders).
- From 1 December 2026, live instructor-led training counts toward the 35 hours only if delivered by a PMI Authorized Training Partner, a China Registered Education Provider, or an eligible accredited higher-education program.
- Renewal: 60 PDUs every 3 years.
- Fees: set by PMI, lower for members; not verified here, do not state amounts.

## PL-300 Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst. Source: Microsoft Learn study guide (skills current as of April 20, 2026) and certification page
- Certification: Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate.
- 100 minutes. Passing score 700 (scaled). Proctored via Pearson VUE; may include interactive components. Microsoft does not publish a fixed question count; do not state one.
- Renew every 12 months with a free online assessment on Microsoft Learn.
- Retake after 24 hours following a first failed attempt.
- Skill areas: Prepare the data 25–30%; Model the data 25–30%; Visualize and analyze the data 25–30%; Manage and secure Power BI 15–20%.
- Prepare the data: get or connect to data (identify and connect to data sources or a shared semantic model; change data source settings incl. credentials and privacy levels; choose between DirectLake, DirectQuery and Import; create and modify parameters); profile and clean (evaluate data incl. statistics and column properties; resolve inconsistencies, unexpected or null values, data quality issues; resolve import errors); transform and load (data types; create and transform columns; group and aggregate rows; pivot, unpivot, transpose; convert semi-structured data to a table; create fact and dimension tables; reference vs duplicate queries; merge and append; keys for relationships; configure data loading).
- Model the data: design (table and column properties; role-playing dimensions; cardinality and cross-filter direction; common date table; use cases for calculated columns and calculated tables); DAX (single aggregation measures; CALCULATE; time intelligence; basic statistical functions; semi-additive measures; quick measures; calculated tables or columns; calculation groups); optimize (remove unnecessary rows and columns; identify poor measures, relationships and visuals with Performance Analyzer and DAX query view; reduce granularity).
- Visualize and analyze: create reports (select visual; format; narrative visual with Copilot; themes; conditional formatting; slicing and filtering; Copilot new report page and suggestions; configure page; when to use a paginated report; visual calculations with DAX); usability and storytelling (bookmarks; custom tooltips; interactions; navigation; sorting; sync slicers; Selection pane grouping and layering; drillthrough incl. pages, filters, buttons; export settings; mobile design; personalization; accessibility; automatic page refresh); patterns and trends (Analyze feature; grouping, binning, clustering; AI visuals; reference lines, error bars, forecasting; outliers and anomalies; Copilot to summarize the semantic model).
- Manage and secure: workspaces and assets (create and configure workspace; configure and update an app; publish, import, update items; dashboards; choose distribution method; subscriptions and data alerts; promote or certify; when a gateway is required; scheduled refresh); secure and govern (workspace roles; item-level access; access to semantic models; RLS roles; RLS group membership; sensitivity labels).

## CISSP (ISC2). Source: ISC2 CISSP exam outline (effective April 15, 2024), ISC2 Exam Ready booklet, cross-checked
- English exams use Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT): 100–150 items, 3 hours, passing score 700 out of 1000. You cannot go back to earlier questions.
- Domains and weights: 1 Security and Risk Management 16%; 2 Asset Security 10%; 3 Security Architecture and Engineering 13%; 4 Communication and Network Security 13%; 5 Identity and Access Management (IAM) 13%; 6 Security Assessment and Testing 12%; 7 Security Operations 13%; 8 Software Development Security 10%.
- Experience: at least five years cumulative, full-time, paid work in two or more of the eight domains. One year can be waived with a four-year degree (or regional equivalent) or an approved credential.
- Without the experience, passing earns Associate of ISC2; you then have six years to gain the required experience.
- Endorsement by an ISC2-certified professional in good standing is required after passing.
- Maintenance: 120 CPE credits per 3-year cycle (40 per year) and an annual maintenance fee (do not state an amount).

## ITIL Foundation (Version 5) (PeopleCert / itil.com)
- Exam: 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes, closed book, pass mark 65% (26 of 40). Online proctored available. No mandatory training prerequisite. Nine languages.
- Renewal every 3 years (CPD route or retake).
- ITIL 4 Foundation holders can take the Foundation Bridge (Version 5).
- Learning objectives: digital product and service management concepts; value co-creation; the four dimensions of product and service management; the ITIL Value System; the ITIL guiding principles; the product and service lifecycle; ITIL's management practices; continual improvement; value stream mapping and management.
- Four dimensions: Organizations and People; Information and Technology; Partners and Suppliers; Value Streams and Processes.
- ITIL Value System components: guiding principles, governance, value chain activities, management practices, continual improvement.
- Seven guiding principles (titles unchanged from ITIL 4): Focus on value; Start where you are; Progress iteratively with feedback; Collaborate and promote visibility; Think and work holistically; Keep it simple and practical; Optimize and automate.
- Eight-stage product and service lifecycle (replaces ITIL 4's six-activity service value chain as the organizing model): Discover, Design, Acquire, Build, Transition, Operate, Deliver, Support.
- Version 5 is described as "AI-native" (AI use and AI governance throughout) and unifies digital product and service management.
- Management practices: ITIL 4 had 34; Version 5 keeps the practices with adjustments and new groupings. Do not state a Version 5 count.
- Continual improvement: Version 5 includes a refined continual improvement model. The ITIL 4 model asks seven questions (What is the vision? Where are we now? Where do we want to be? How do we get there? Take action. Did we get there? How do we keep the momentum going?). Present these as the ITIL 4 model that Version 5 refines.
- Do NOT use ITIL 4 value chain activity names (plan, improve, engage, design and transition, obtain/build, deliver and support) as if they were Version 5 terms.
